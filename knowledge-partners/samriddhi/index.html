NUNP is proud to recognize Samriddhi Wealth Creation as a Knowledge Partner in the area of Financial Literacy.

About the Partner

About Samriddhi Wealth Creation

Samriddhi Wealth Creation is dedicated to helping individuals and families build long-term financial well-being through structured financial planning, disciplined investing, and continuous financial education.

The organization emphasizes clarity, planning, and informed decision-making, encouraging individuals to align financial choices with their life goals rather than making short-term or impulsive decisions. Its educational initiatives promote practical financial understanding, responsible money management, and long-term financial confidence.

Key Contributions

Signature Knowledge Contributions

Mind Money Mastery

Mind Money Mastery explores the relationship between mindset, financial behaviour, and long-term financial success.

The initiative encourages participants to:

  • Develop healthy financial habits.
  • Understand the psychology of money.
  • Build financial discipline.
  • Make thoughtful financial decisions.
  • Align financial choices with personal and professional goals.

Business Financial Clarity

Business Financial Clarity focuses on helping entrepreneurs and business owners better understand the financial side of running a business.

Key areas include:

  • Understanding business cash flow.
  • Separating personal and business finances.
  • Financial planning for business sustainability.
  • Building financial confidence and identifying blind spots.
  • Making informed business decisions.

* The programme is designed as an educational experience that promotes financial awareness rather than investment advice or product recommendations.

Partnership Principles

The collaboration between NUNP and Samriddhi Wealth Creation is intended solely for educational and knowledge-sharing purposes. The partnership focuses on promoting financial literacy, responsible financial behaviour, and informed decision-making. It does not constitute an endorsement of specific financial products, investment schemes, or advisory services.
