Collaboration Profile

Founding Partner

Pride Academy (PACE) is the founding Knowledge Partner of NUNP, contributing expertise in Prevention of Sexual Harassment (PoSH) and promoting safe, respectful, and inclusive environments.

Through this partnership, PACE supports NUNP by sharing knowledge, educational resources, and awareness that help students and young professionals understand workplace ethics, respectful behaviour, gender sensitivity, professional conduct, and legal awareness.

Strategic Impact

Why This Partnership Matters

"Together, NUNP and PACE aim to cultivate responsible citizens who uphold dignity, respect, and accountability in educational institutions, workplaces, and society."

This partnership reflects a shared commitment to empowering young people with the knowledge, values, and awareness needed to create respectful campuses, ethical workplaces, and responsible communities. By bridging the gap between legal guidelines and real-world behavior, this collaboration builds a foundation for long-term safety and equality.
