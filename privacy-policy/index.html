NUNP – நம்ம ஊர் நம்ம பொறுப்பு (“NUNP”, “we”, “our”, or “us”) is a citizen-led community development initiative focused on youth leadership, volunteerism, civic participation and community transformation.

This Privacy Policy explains how NUNP collects, uses, stores, protects and handles personal information when you visit or interact with the NUNP website, participate in our programmes, volunteer for our activities, submit an enquiry, or otherwise communicate with us.

By using the NUNP website or voluntarily providing your personal information to us, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

1 INFORMATION WE MAY COLLECT

Depending on how you interact with NUNP, we may collect the following information:

1.1 Information You Provide

You may voluntarily provide information such as:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Mobile/telephone number
  • City, locality or general location
  • Age or age group, where relevant
  • Educational details
  • Institution/college details
  • Professional/employment details
  • Areas of interest
  • Volunteer availability & interests
  • Programme/application info
  • Enquiry & feedback form entries

We will collect only information that is reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose.

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

When you visit our website, certain technical information may be collected automatically, depending on the website technologies and services we use. This may include:

  • • IP address
  • • Browser type
  • • Device type
  • • Operating system
  • • Pages visited
  • • Date & time of access
  • • Referring website
  • • Website usage information
  • • Cookies & similar technologies

This information may be used to maintain website security, understand website usage and improve the website experience.

2 HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION

NUNP may use personal information for legitimate and specified purposes, including:

Responding to enquiries and requests
Processing programme registrations & applications
Managing volunteers & community participants
Communicating schedules, activities & updates
Youth Leadership Fellowship management
Coordinating community development activities
Connecting volunteers with opportunities
Conducting leadership & development programmes
Sending information about NUNP initiatives
Collecting feedback & improving programmes
Maintaining participation & programme records
Communicating administrative information
Preventing misuse, fraud or unauthorised activity
Maintaining website security & functionality
Complying with legal & regulatory requirements
Supporting administration of NUNP initiatives

NUNP will not use personal information for purposes that are materially different from the purpose for which it was collected unless permitted by applicable law or otherwise appropriately communicated.

3 VOLUNTEER AND PROGRAMME INFORMATION

NUNP may conduct initiatives involving:

Youth leadership Community volunteering Community development projects Leadership development Fellowship programmes Volunteer Saturdays Business & community outreach Other civic participation initiatives

If you register or apply for any such initiative, we may use the information you provide to assess your participation, communicate with you, coordinate activities and maintain appropriate programme records.

Participation in NUNP programmes is voluntary unless otherwise stated.

4 COMMUNICATIONS

If you provide your contact information to NUNP, we may contact you regarding:

  • • Your enquiry
  • • Your application or registration
  • • Programme-related information
  • • Volunteer opportunities
  • • Event information
  • • Changes or updates relating to an activity
  • • Other relevant NUNP communications

Where consent is required for promotional or non-essential communications, we will seek appropriate consent.

You may request that we stop sending certain non-essential communications.

5 COOKIES AND SIMILAR TECHNOLOGIES

The NUNP website may use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • • Enable essential website functionality
  • • Improve website performance
  • • Understand how visitors use the website
  • • Remember certain preferences
  • • Improve user experience
  • • Support website security

Where applicable, third-party services used on the website may also place cookies or collect technical information in accordance with their own privacy policies.

You may be able to control or disable cookies through your browser settings. However, disabling certain cookies may affect some website functionality.

6 SHARING OF PERSONAL INFORMATION

NUNP does NOT sell or rent your personal information.

We may share personal information only where reasonably necessary for legitimate purposes, including with:

  • Service providers supporting website hosting, technology, communications or data management
  • Programme partners or organisations involved in delivering a specific initiative, where necessary
  • Individuals or organisations involved in coordinating a programme or community activity, where appropriate
  • Professional advisers where reasonably necessary
  • Government authorities, regulators or law-enforcement agencies where required by applicable law
  • Other parties where you have provided appropriate consent or where disclosure is otherwise permitted by law

We expect service providers handling personal information on our behalf to take appropriate measures to protect that information.

7 DATA SECURITY

NUNP takes reasonable measures to protect personal information against:

Unauthorised access Unauthorised disclosure Loss Misuse Alteration Destruction

We use reasonable organisational, technical and administrative safeguards appropriate to the nature of the information and the purposes for which it is processed.

However, no method of transmitting or storing information electronically can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8 RETENTION OF PERSONAL INFORMATION

NUNP will retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, for legitimate organisational requirements, or as required by applicable law.

When personal information is no longer required, we may delete, anonymise or otherwise appropriately dispose of it, subject to applicable legal and operational requirements.

Different categories of information may therefore be retained for different periods.

9 YOUR RIGHTS

Subject to applicable law and any conditions or limitations prescribed under it, individuals may have rights relating to their personal information, including rights to:

  • Request information about the personal data being processed
  • Request correction or updating of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of personal information where applicable
  • Withdraw consent where consent is the basis for processing
  • Raise a grievance regarding the processing of personal information
  • Exercise other rights available under applicable data protection law

Requests may be submitted using the contact details provided in Section 15 of this Privacy Policy. NUNP may need to verify the identity of the person making a request before processing it.

Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (India)

The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 provides for rights including access to information, correction/erasure and grievance redressal, subject to the applicable provisions of the law.

10 CHILDREN AND YOUNG PERSONS

Some NUNP initiatives may involve students and young people.

Where an activity involves children or persons for whom parental or guardian consent is legally required, NUNP will take appropriate steps as required by applicable law.

We do not knowingly seek unnecessary personal information from children.

Where personal information relating to a child is collected for a legitimate programme purpose, such information will be handled in accordance with applicable legal requirements and appropriate safeguards.

11 THIRD-PARTY WEBSITES AND SERVICES

The NUNP website may contain links to external websites, social media platforms or other third-party services.

NUNP is not responsible for the privacy practices, security or content of external websites.

We encourage users to review the privacy policies of third-party websites before providing them with personal information.

12 PHOTOGRAPHS, VIDEOS AND STORIES

NUNP may document community activities, programmes, events and initiatives through photographs, videos, testimonials or stories for purposes such as:

Where required, NUNP will seek appropriate permission or consent before using identifiable personal content for such purposes.

If you believe that your photograph, video, testimonial or other identifiable content has been used inappropriately, you may contact us using the details provided below.

NUNP may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect:

  • • Changes in programmes/activities
  • • Changes in technology
  • • Changes in data practices
  • • Changes in laws or regulations
  • • Changes in data protection rules

We encourage you to review this page periodically.

Any matters relating to personal data will be handled in accordance with applicable Indian data protection and information technology laws and regulations.
