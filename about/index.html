About NUNP

NUNP (நம்ம ஊர் நம்ம பொறுப்பு) is a citizen-led community movement committed to developing responsible leaders and building stronger communities.

Rather than waiting for others to solve problems, we encourage citizens to identify issues, organize people, and create sustainable local solutions.

Our movement welcomes students, professionals, entrepreneurs, homemakers, senior citizens, and anyone willing to contribute positively to society.
